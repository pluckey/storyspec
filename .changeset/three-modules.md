`storyspec ui` is reorganised around three questions. Its tabs become a left navigation that lands on Needs you, one list of everything waiting on a person with its action inline (Decide, Approve, Check again, Check, Claim), above a home that says what each persona can do today and what is being built; then Model (what should be: People, Stories, Decisions, Records, Words), Implementation (what is: Code, Ports, Layers) and Proof (how we know: By tests, By a run, By a person, Stale). The trace's findings sit on the screen of the thing they name instead of one Findings tab. Every state is worked out on the server from the same scan, trace and proof as the CLI and opens into why, with the action that would change it (a stale proof shows the text then and now, from git). Stories fold to one line each with a proof summary, and the URL keeps the screen, the open stories and the filter. A person can record a manual check (holds or fails, with a note) on the page, written to `stories/PROOF.json` by the same function as `storyspec prove`, and claim unclaimed code for a story's `implementedBy:`. Every word on the page comes from one exported vocabulary table, with IDs, versions, rule names and hashes kept to a details line.
